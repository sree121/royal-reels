        How to play

        • Every game gives you 10 spins. Pull the lever, swipe down on the cards, press SPIN or hit the spacebar to start.
        • Take your call (red button): tap it — or hold it — to rev up the shuffle and the reels, up to ×3. Keep tapping to keep them fast; stop and they slow back down. Tap between spins to charge up the next one. It never changes your chances.
        • Every spin is freshly shuffled. Each reel is its own deck, shuffled fairly with secure random numbers before every spin, so no run can be predicted.
        • You decide when to stop — luck decides the cards. The reels keep spinning until you tap the reels, press STOP, hit the spacebar or pull the lever again. Each reel then coasts a random distance and settles, so every spin is a fresh lucky draw.
        • 3 of the same in any line (across, down or corner to corner): KKK = 1,000 (jackpot) · QQQ = 400 · ♥♥♥ = 250 · JJJ = 250 · ♦♦♦ = 180 · ♣♣♣ = 120 · ♠♠♠ = 80.
        • Royal Line: J, Q, K in order along a line (J → Q → K, or K → Q → J) = 200.
        • 2 + 1 suits — two cards of one suit side by side plus one of another suit, on any of the 8 lines (♥ ♥ ♠ and ♠ ♥ ♥ score, ♥ ♠ ♥ doesn't). Each mix has its own score, from ♥♥ + ♦ = 25 down to ♠♠ + ♣ = 5 (see the grid in Points). J, Q and K only score as a set of 3.
        • Every spin is a lucky draw — the odds reshuffle each time, so any suit can come in a streak. All four suits are equally likely; J, Q and K are rarer, the King rarest. Face-down cards (▒) never match.
        • ♥ meter: it fills with the points you score. Reach 200, 500, 900, 1,400, 2,000… points in a game and each one gives you 3 extra spins. Keep scoring to keep going!
        • Bigger high score, more spins: every 400 points your high score is above 500 adds 1 spin to each game (up to +5).
        • When your spins run out, your final score is checked against the high score (at least 500). Beat it and the King, Queen and Joker come out to dance!

        16-bit, Terminal and Classic are three looks for the same game — switch any time without losing your spins.
