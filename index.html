        How to play

        • Every game gives you 10 spins. Wind the wheel like a spring — the power ring fills as you turn it, up to 3 full turns — then let go. The spring unwinds and throws the reels: the more you wound, the faster and longer they spin. (A quick tap winds one turn for you; the lever, a swipe down on the cards and the spacebar also spin.)
        • Take your call (red button): tap it — or hold it — to rev up the shuffle and the reels, up to ×3. Keep tapping to keep them fast; stop and they slow back down. Tap between spins to charge up the next one. It never changes your chances.
        • Every spin is freshly shuffled. Each reel is its own deck, shuffled fairly with secure random numbers before every spin, so no run can be predicted.
        • Your hands decide the cards. Leave the reels alone and they slow down by themselves and click onto cards when the spring's force is spent — the left reel first. Or grab the wheel and hold it to slow them down sooner, like a hand on a spinning wheel; let go and they coast on with what's left. The spacebar or the lever stops them with a slow slide. No hidden tricks.
        • 3 of the same in any line (across, down or corner to corner): KKK = 1,000 (jackpot) · QQQ = 400 · ♥♥♥ = 250 · JJJ = 250 · ♦♦♦ = 180 · ♣♣♣ = 120 · ♠♠♠ = 80.
        • Royal Line: J, Q, K in order along a line (J → Q → K, or K → Q → J) = 200.
        • 2 + 1 suits — two cards of one suit side by side plus one of another suit, on any of the 8 lines (♥ ♥ ♠ and ♠ ♥ ♥ score, ♥ ♠ ♥ doesn't). Each mix has its own score, from ♥♥ + ♦ = 25 down to ♠♠ + ♣ = 5 (see the grid in Points). J, Q and K only score as a set of 3.
        • The odds are the same on every spin: all four suits are equally likely; J, Q and K are rarer, the King rarest; 1 card in 10 is face-down (▒) and never matches.
        • ♥ meter: it fills with the points you score. Reach 200, 500, 900, 1,400, 2,000… points in a game and each one gives you 3 extra spins. Keep scoring to keep going!
        • Bigger high score, more spins: every 400 points your high score is above 500 adds 1 spin to each game (up to +5).
        • When your spins run out, your final score is checked against the high score (at least 500). Beat it and the King, Queen and Joker come out to dance!

        16-bit, Terminal and Classic are three looks for the same game — switch any time without losing your spins.

        How to play roulette

        • Chips: you start with 1,000 chips. Pick a chip value (1, 5, 25, 100 or 500), then tap the table to place it. Tap again to add more. If you run out, take another 1,000.
        • Bets: tap a number for a straight up bet. Tap the line between two numbers for a split, a corner where four meet, or the outer edge of a row for a street (three) or six line (six). The outside boxes cover red/black, odd/even, 1–18/19–36, the dozens and the "2 to 1" columns.
        • Spin: flick the wheel with your finger or mouse, or press SPIN (or the spacebar). You can keep betting while the ball circles, until the call "No more bets".
        • Winning: the ball settles in a pocket and a crown marker goes on that number. Winning bets are paid with your stake back; losing chips are swept away. Use Rebet to place the same bets again, or Double to double them.
        • Pays: straight up 35 to 1 · split 17 · street 11 · corner 8 · six line 5 · dozen or column 2 · red/black, odd/even, low/high 1 to 1. First four (0-1-2-3) pays 8; on the American wheel the top line (0-00-1-2-3) pays 6.
        • Wheels: European has one zero (house edge 2.7%). French adds La Partage: when zero wins you get half of your even-money bets back. American has 0 and 00 (house edge 5.26%).
        • Call bets (European and French): Voisins du Zéro, Tiers du Cylindre, Orphelins, Jeu Zéro and Neighbours cover whole sections of the wheel.
        • Fair play: the pocket is drawn with secure random numbers when the ball is launched. Every pocket has exactly the same chance, and nothing depends on past spins or your bets. It's play money only.
